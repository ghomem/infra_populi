## Behaviour findings (escalations/firewall_secure_extra.behaviour.md)
Both are baseline defects of rule order, not migration effects.
- **The blacklist** (rule 666, TCP) comes after every rule that accepts TCP, so a blacklisted source keeps SSH.
- **The RST limit** (519/520) comes after 003 (established accept). RSTs outside a connection are INVALID and dropped
  by 501 first, so no RST ever reaches 519 or 520.
